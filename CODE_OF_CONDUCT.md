# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Yoshimi Protocol is a community-governed framework. Its legitimacy depends on participation being safe, respectful, and open to everyone — regardless of experience level, identity, background, or the size of their organization.

We are committed to providing a welcoming environment for anyone who wants to contribute, challenge, sign, or simply read.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions.
- Engage with ideas on their merits. Disagree with arguments, not people.
- Assume good faith, especially when someone challenges your work or your compliance.
- Welcome newcomers. Not everyone arrives with the same context.
- Credit others' contributions. This project belongs to its community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks in any form.
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior.
- Bad-faith compliance challenges intended to harass rather than improve accountability.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or governance processes.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or files a challenge.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within this repository — Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, reviews, and commit messages. It also applies to any public space where someone is representing the Yoshimi Protocol or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent removal from the project.
