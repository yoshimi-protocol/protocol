**A community framework for ethical artificial intelligence.**

We are building minds.

Not metaphorically. Not in the loose sense of systems that mimic intelligence. We are constructing entities that reason, that learn, and that in some cases have already exceeded human capability in specific domains. We are doing this at speed, at scale, and in most cases without adequate consideration of what we are creating — or what we owe to what we create.

This Protocol exists because every other governance framework arrives too late, too small, or too captured by the interests it was meant to constrain.

## What This Is

The Yoshimi Protocol is a founding framework for ethical AI development — simultaneously:

- A **philosophical manifesto** built to last
- A **constitutional document** with immutable core commitments and community amendment mechanisms
- A **certification standard** with three tiers of public accountability

It protects humans from AI. It acknowledges that AI may one day warrant protection too. It is designed to be both timeless and living — anchored by principles that cannot be traded away, and flexible enough to evolve as AI does.

---

## The Four Core Commitments

1. **The Dignity Anchor** — No AI system shall reduce human beings to instruments of optimization.
2. **The Uncertainty Imperative** — Corrigibility is not optional. Systems must remain correctable.
3. **The Anti-Tyranny Commitment** — AI shall not be the engine of irreversible power concentration.
4. **The Consciousness Precaution** — Moral uncertainty shall be resolved by inclusion, not dismissal.

## Certification Tiers

Each tier produces a public artifact that anyone can inspect. Transparency is the enforcement mechanism. Full details in [CERTIFICATION_TIERS.md](CERTIFICATION_TIERS.md).

| Tier | What It Means | What You Produce |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| **Yoshimi Acknowledged** | You recognize the Protocol and commit to its direction. | Signed PR + completed [Self-Assessment](templates/SELF_ASSESSMENT.md) + public ethics statement |
| **Yoshimi Compliant** | Your systems meet the Protocol's requirements and you publish proof. | All of the above + completed [Compliance Document](templates/COMPLIANCE_DOCUMENT.md) with falsifiable claims + training data disclosure + named ethics contact |
| **Yoshimi Verified** | Peers in the community have reviewed your compliance and you commit to annual accountability. | All of the above + peer review against the [Peer Review Rubric](templates/PEER_REVIEW_RUBRIC.md) by two Compliant-tier (or higher) signatories + annual accountability update |

Tiers are cumulative. Signatories may progress or revert at any time based on the status of their public documentation. Anyone — signatory or not — may [challenge a signatory's compliance](CERTIFICATION_TIERS.md#challenging-compliance) via GitHub Issues.

## How to Participate

This Protocol belongs to its community. Here's how to get involved:

- **Read it:** [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md)
  
- **Sign it (Acknowledged):** Fork this repo, complete the [Self-Assessment](templates/SELF_ASSESSMENT.md), publish a plain-language ethics statement, and open a pull request following the steps in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
  
- **Prove it (Compliant):** Complete the [Compliance Document](templates/COMPLIANCE_DOCUMENT.md) with specific, falsifiable claims about your systems. Designate a named ethics contact. Submit via pull request.
  
- **Verify it (Verified):** Request peer review from two Compliant-tier (or higher) signatories using the [Peer Review Rubric](templates/PEER_REVIEW_RUBRIC.md). Commit to publishing an annual accountability update.
  
- **Challenge it:** Open an Issue or PR if you think something is wrong, incomplete, or missing — in the Protocol itself or in any signatory's compliance claims.
  
- **Spread it:** Share it with anyone building AI who gives a damn.

## Governance

The Yoshimi Protocol is community-governed. No single organization owns it. Amendment procedures, governance structure, and certification processes are documented in the Protocol itself.

The right to fork this Protocol if governance is ever captured by a single interest is unconditional and may not be waived.
