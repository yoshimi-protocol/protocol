# Yoshimi Peer Review Rubric

**Template for Verified Tier Peer Reviews**

*This rubric is used by Compliant-tier (or higher) signatories to evaluate another signatory's Compliance Document as part of the Verified tier process. Reviews are public documents linked to both the reviewer's and the reviewee's signatory records.*

*A peer review is not an audit. You are not certifying that the signatory's systems are flawless. You are evaluating whether their Compliance Document is honest, specific, and substantive — whether it makes claims that are falsifiable and consistent with observable practice, and whether it engages seriously with each of the four commitments.*

*Anonymous reviews are not accepted. Your name and signatory status are part of the review's credibility.*

## Review Criteria

For each section, assign one of three ratings:

- **Meets Standard** — The section contains specific, falsifiable claims that engage seriously with the commitment. Gaps and limitations are acknowledged. The reviewer has no material concerns.
- **Meets Standard with Observations** — The section is substantive but the reviewer has identified areas for improvement, clarification, or further development. These observations are documented below. This rating does not block Verified status.
- **Does Not Meet Standard** — The section lacks specificity, makes claims that are not falsifiable, omits material information, or does not engage seriously with the commitment. The reviewer's concerns are documented below. This rating blocks Verified status for the section in question.

A Compliance Document must receive **Meets Standard** or **Meets Standard with Observations** on all four commitment sections and the Training Data Disclosure from both reviewers to achieve Verified status.

## Post-Review Process

After both peer reviews are submitted:

- If both reviewers recommend **Approve** or **Approve with Observations**, the signatory's status is updated to Verified and the reviews are linked to their signatory record.
- If either reviewer recommends **Request Revisions**, the signatory revises the identified sections and resubmits. The same reviewers evaluate the revisions unless they are unavailable, in which case new reviewers may be assigned.
- If the signatory disagrees with a reviewer's assessment, they may respond publicly and request a third reviewer. The third reviewer's recommendation resolves the disagreement.

All reviews, responses, and revisions are public.
